# MAC — Modular Architecture & Connectivity

Independent data engineering and custom business software demonstration inspired by public consulting principles. No affiliation or endorsement implied.

## Objective
Understand business workflows, connect existing tools, engineer reliable data and deliver a custom operating layer.

## Demonstration scope
Proposed fictional wholesale distributor; synthetic records and explicitly simulated external APIs. Real file ingestion, SQL transformations, validation, reconciliation and monitoring. No hardcoded engineering results.

## Design
Approved dark enterprise command centre: left navigation, central operations and engineering metrics, right copilot. Responsive layout. Public pages must not mention job applications or employment objectives.

## Controls
No public deployment, external live integrations or outreach without approval. Keep secrets and personal data out of source control. Use isolated development branches and shared tracker.
